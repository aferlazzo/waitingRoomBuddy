Three steps to your app

  1. Download the app

    Tap the green button above on your Android phone. If your browser asks whether to keep or download the file, confirm ThinkAgain.apk.

  2. Open the file and install

    Tap ThinkAgain.apk in Downloads, then Install. Android may ask for permission or a security scan; the help below explains those screens.

  3. Put ThinkAgain on your home screen

    Tap Open after installation. In ThinkAgain, choose Add home-screen icon and approve Android's prompt. Tap the new icon to start thinking.

If Android asks…

“Not allowed to install apps from this source”

Tap Settings on that prompt, turn on Allow from this source for the browser or Files app you used, then return and tap Install. This permission lets that app open the installer.

Play Protect wants to scan the app

Choose Scan app if offered and let the check finish. Continue with Install if Android permits it. If it explicitly blocks the app or reports it as harmful, stop and send Tony a screenshot of the message. Keep Play Protect enabled.

The download finished, but nothing opened

Open Files by Google → Downloads and tap ThinkAgain.apk. You need the file ending in .apk. A file ending in .zip is not this installer.

It still says “Downloading”

In Chrome, open ⋮ → Downloads and check the entry for ThinkAgain.apk. If it is paused, tap resume. If it is stuck, cancel that entry and retry the green button using a working internet connection.

Installed, but I can't find the icon

Go to your phone's home screen and swipe up to see all apps. Find ThinkAgain, then press and hold its icon and drag it onto your home screen. Once installed, you open that icon—not the downloaded file.

The link opened inside my email app

Use that app's menu to choose Open in Chrome or Open in browser, then tap the download button on this page.
